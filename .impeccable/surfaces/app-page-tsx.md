## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is the entrance hall of a daylight cellar, not a dark luxury boutique. Pale champagne-green light on chalk walls, one gold light source, bottles standing in it. It refuses the prestige-champagne category default: black field, gold serif, full-bleed carousel. Chromatic weight replaces theatrical darkness; the room is bright, quiet and exact.

OWN-WORLD: A 60/30/10 field — chalk `#FBFAF6`/`#FFFFFF` for reading surfaces, champagne green in four depths (`#E6EFDD` hero veil, `#EFF3E8` pillar band, `#D9E4CC` footer, `#C6D6B5` announcement rail) for atmosphere, and champagne gold `#E3C47D` held to accents only (primary CTA, seals, hairlines, halo). Text is ink `#14150F`; eyebrows are bronze `#755A22`; borders are green ink `#2E4636` at 10–35 % alpha. Cormorant Garamond in light weights for display, Geist for body, 9–10 px uppercase micro-labels tracked 0.2–0.3 em. Zero radius except circular seals; depth from hairlines and one radial halo, never stacked shadows.

STORY: The visitor understands within one screen that this is a confidential, cellar-narrated selection assembled in Épernay, documented bottle by bottle rather than merchandised by discount. They believe the house is quiet, exact and expensive in the right way. They act by opening the collection or a single cuvée dossier, then configuring a bottle in the selection drawer.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A centred composition on the green veil: 9 px bronze eyebrow, a Cormorant headline at clamp 2.25→6 rem in light weight with one italic bronze phrase, a single 2xl-max paragraph at 60ch in `#333A2F`, then a gold primary CTA beside a transposed ghost link. The section holds 82 vh with a radial gold halo behind the type; above it the cream navbar carries the green announcement rail. The gold CTA is the only saturated mass in the first 1000 px, so the eye lands on the bottle photography that follows, not on the chrome.

FORM: Editorial single-column gallery with hairline-separated bands, code-led build. Position 2 of the ordered list: photographic gallery re-sequenced as a daylight cellar rather than a night vault. seed:f8e958d947aaf44e.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
